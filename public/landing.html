What it does

A book first. Tools when you need them.

CookCap ships as a hardcover you flip — not a recipe dump with chrome bolted on.

Hardcover flip book

Leather cover, title page, chapters, and paper tabs on a wooden reading desk — with Home, ±5 jump, and a page scrubber.

Cook mode

Start cooking for step-by-step guidance. Optional lenses — My Plate, Mother, Budget, Ramadan, and more — stay additive.

Smart search

Find dishes by name or phrase — “30 min yogurt”, occasions, vegetarian rails — without leaving the book.

Shopping & planner

Build a shopping list and a week plan from recipes you actually open — stored on your device.

Family edition

Name the book {Name} Cooks. Favorites, notes, guest PIN, and merge backup keep the household edition coherent.

Private by design

No account, no ads, no analytics SDK. Your notes, photos, and plans stay on the device.
