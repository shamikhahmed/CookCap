CookCap Privacy

Effective 25 October 2026 · Version 3.7.8 · Capricorn Systems

Summary

CookCap is an offline-first family cookbook. Your notes, favorites, meal plans, and photos stay on your device. There are no accounts, analytics SDKs, ads, or trackers in the app.

What stays on your device

  • Book name and preferences (theme, skin, lenses)
  • Favorites, notes, shopping list, meal plan, pantry, diary
  • Imported recipes and hero / cover photos you add
  • Profiles and guest PIN (when used)

What leaves your device

ServiceWhatWhen
GitHub PagesStatic app shell + bundled recipe assetsOn load / update

CookCap does not call third-party analytics, ad, or identity APIs.

Nutrition

Nutrition values shown in the app are estimates for cooking guidance only — not medical or dietary advice.

Children

CookCap is a general cooking tool. Do not enter personal data about children beyond what you choose to keep locally for household cooking.
