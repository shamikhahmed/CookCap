CookCap Privacy

Effective 15 September 2026 · Capricorn Systems

Summary

CookCap is an offline-first family cookbook. Your notes, favorites, meal plans, and photos stay on your device. No accounts or analytics SDKs ship in the app.

What stays on your device

  • Book name and preferences
  • Favorites, notes, shopping list, meal plan
  • Imported recipes and bottle/photo heroes you add
  • Profiles / pantry / diary (when used)
